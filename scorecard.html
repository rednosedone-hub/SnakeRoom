Pair & Clutch Scorecard

Every pairing graded on what it has actually produced: eggs laid, eggs that hatched, hatchlings worth keeping and sales. The grade is the pooled healthy hatch rate across all of that pairing's hatched clutches — eggs in, clean hatchlings out, nothing weighted behind the scenes. A hatchling with a recorded defect (shark mouth, no eyes, kink, bug eyes, small eyes) still counts as hatched, but it does not count toward the grade. A pairing with a single clutch is marked as a first result, not a settled reputation.

Sales per clutch is the money side: realised sales divided by hatched clutches, before costs, so it is revenue rather than profit. A pairing marked as a project is read differently — its keepers are the progress toward the target morph, and sales are only the surplus.

Projects

Pairings that name the same target morph are read as steps of one project: what each step hatched, whether it reached the goal, and what the next move is. Record the step number, outcome and next move on the pair (or its clutch card) on the Breeding page.

Pairings

Working pairings sit at the top, the ones to question next, then pairings with no hatch result yet. A clutch recorded with both parents is matched to its pairing even when the old record was never linked by hand. Set a pairing's purpose, target morph, step number, outcome and next move on the Breeding page; a converted pair keeps them all on its clutch card. A project step is judged on the morph it produced, not on how many eggs hatched.

Clutches

Each clutch scored on its own: eggs in, hatchlings out, what did not hatch, and what the hatchlings sold for. Money is only counted once an animal is marked Sold or given a sale date, never from an asking price.
