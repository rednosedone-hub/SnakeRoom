The method

Bred to a plan, not a lottery

Three or four pairings a season, chosen because the offspring are worth raising — not because the combination sounds impressive.

01

Selective pairing

Each clutch targets a specific look or a specific project step. Breeders are chosen on structure, feeding response and long-term health first, colour second.

02

Records, not memory

Every animal lives in The Snake Room — our breeding software. Weights, feeds, sheds, pairings and hatch dates are logged as they happen, so what you are told about an animal is what was recorded.

03

Health before colour

Animals are quarantined before they are listed. Spider-complex genes are paired deliberately, never as a homozygous cross, and any wobble is disclosed up front.
