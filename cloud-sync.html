Account


                

                    Preferred: sign in with email and password. Create yourself as a user in the
                    Supabase dashboard under Authentication → Users → Add user, set a
                    password there, and tick "Auto Confirm User". This needs no email at all.
                


                

                    Staying signed in: your session renews itself automatically, so a normal
                    sign-in lasts indefinitely. The usual way to get logged out is the browser
                    wiping site data — iPhone Safari does this after a week of not
                    visiting a site. On a phone, use Share → Add to Home Screen
                    and open The Snake Room from there: installed apps keep their data for
                    good. And if a browser ever wipes it, recovering only needs your password
                    — the connection is built in and your collection pulls automatically.
