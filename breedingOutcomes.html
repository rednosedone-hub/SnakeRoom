Record a Past Clutch

For a clutch that hatched before you logged it. The parents and the hatch date are enough; the laid date and egg count can stay blank. Hatchlings already in your collection whose ID carries this clutch's code (SS-C25-1-01 and so on) are linked to it automatically, so lineage and Season Review see the family. Purchased animals keep their own SS-### codes and are never renumbered.

Clutches To Hatch

When a clutch hatches, review the hatchlings here and turn them into real animals in your collection. Each hatchling gets a name, bin number, sex, and status. Pick the morph it hatched as and its genes are saved with it, so the calculator, breeding records and the Morph Gallery all agree on what it is.
