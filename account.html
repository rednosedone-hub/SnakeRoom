Account Status

Checking connection…

Everything you change saves to the cloud on its own a few seconds after you make it, and comes back on any device you sign in to. Changes are merged record by record, so edits to different snakes, pairs, clutches or expenses on two devices are all kept. If the same field was changed on both, this device's value is kept and the status below says how many records need a look. There is usually nothing to press.

Sign In
