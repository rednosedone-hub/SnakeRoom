Everything you change saves to the cloud on its own a few seconds after you make it, and comes back on any device you sign in to. Changes are merged record by record, so edits to different snakes, pairs, clutches or expenses on two devices are all kept. If the same field was changed on both, this device's value is kept and the status below says how many records need a look. There is usually nothing to press.

Prices shown in CAD. Cancel or switch plans anytime—your animal records are never deleted if you downgrade.

What happens to my snakes if I downgrade?

Nothing is erased. If you drop below a tier's animal limit, your existing records stay intact and readable—you just won't be able to add new animals until you're back under the cap.

Does the Storefront replace my website?

Yes. Pro gives you a branded public page with live available animals, an embedded morph calculator, buyer enquiry forms, and an education guide generated directly from the genes in your room.

Backup History

Every push keeps the version it replaced, so a bad edit or an accidental restore can be undone. Versions belong to this account only — another keeper can never see them, and neither can the app's owner.
