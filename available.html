This is the private staging list, not the shop. It shows every animal marked Available or On Hold, with the asking price that will be published. Buyers only ever see the Soulless Serpents storefront.

Storefront contact

Buyers write to these details on every listing you publish, so they are required before an animal can go to the storefront. An animal whose export carries no contact is skipped by the publish build.

Push these animals to Soulless Serpents

  1. Fill in the Storefront contact above — animals without it are skipped by the build.
  2. Set each animal's Status to Available and fill its Asking price on the snake's record. On Hold animals publish too, at their agreed price.
  3. Open Data Tools and press Export snakes.
  4. From the repository root, run node site-tools/build-available.js snake-room-collection-YYYY-MM-DD.csv (add --include-sold to keep sold animals as reference).
  5. Upload the soulless-serpents folder to the site.

Photos on an animal that is marked Available are also published to the shared Morph Gallery automatically, filed by morph only — never a name or any record.
